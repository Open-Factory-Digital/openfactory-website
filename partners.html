Certification and partners

OpenFactory is Apache-2.0 software. The OpenFactory name and marks are reserved for people, companies, builds and hosted services that meet the program's published requirements. This page summarises the program. The full documents are in the core repository.

Program documents: openfactory-core/docs/program. Program version 1.0.

Delivery method

How a certified implementation is delivered

Certified partners follow a defined process from first contact to ongoing maintenance. Each phase has entry criteria, deliverables and evidence. The method wraps the platform's own onboarding path; it does not replace the documentation.

PhaseScopeEvidence
0 QualifyWhether to assess: a runnable test command, a route to the model provider, a supported forge, reviewers for the pull requestsQualification note
1 AssessReadiness of the codebase and organisation; cost estimate; prerequisite workenv read, env context, scorecard, assessment report
2 DesignDeployment shape, providers, credentials, box, quality floor, merge policy, post-merge behaviourSigned design record
3 ImplementInstall, onboard, prove, rehearse, first ticket, costed pilot, hand-overdoctor, box status, env rehearse, pilot report, runbook
4 OperateDaily, weekly, monthly and quarterly operator cadencesOperator's log, cost dashboard
5 SupportSeverities, response times by tier, escalation to the maintainersTicket record
6 MaintainUpgrades, proofs, credential rotation, retention, backup and restoreMaintenance calendar

Three profiles

Light: one team, one machine or one small stack. Standard: a hosted forge, a board, several people on one panel. Enterprise: a signed security review, identity integration, a promotion chain with a human production gate, and audit requirements.

All profiles run the same build with every feature. A profile is a set of required controls, each with a defined verification.

Rules that apply to every phase

  • The platform proposes; the customer reviews and merges. Partners do not merge into customer repositories.
  • The box is proven and a rehearsal is costed before any pilot ticket runs. Pilots have a budget cap.
  • Customers are told what the platform does not do, as published on the status page, before the design record is signed.
  • Exit criteria are command output or signed documents.

Certifications

Individual certifications

Seven certifications, one per role. Certifications are held by individuals, expire, and can be verified by certificate number. Hands-on roles are examined in a lab built on the one-machine door with a stub agent, so exams require no model credential.

Associate (OFCA)

For requesters, reviewers and sponsors. Multiple choice, 75 minutes. Valid three years. No prerequisite.

Operator (OFCO)

Runs a deployment: install, diagnose, prove the box, handle parked work, upgrade, back up and restore. Performance-based, two hours. Valid two years.

Implementer (OFCI)

Takes a codebase from assessment to go-live. Performance-based, three hours. Valid two years. Requires a valid Operator certification.

Solution Architect (OFCSA)

Designs deployments and signs security reviews. A graded design record plus a scenario exam. Requires a valid Implementer certification.

Product Owner (OFCPO)

Owns requirements, baseline adoption, confirmations and release. Two hours. Valid three years. No prerequisite.

Developer (OFCD)

Writes add-ons to the extension model and the contribution rules, verified with the conformance suite. Three hours. Valid two years.

Trainer (OFCT)

Delivers the program's courses. Requires Implementer or Solution Architect, an observed teach-back, and annual renewal on delivery records.

Exam domains, policies, courses and proposed fees: docs/program/03-role-certifications.md.

Partner program

Partner tiers

Partners are companies that implement, host, support, teach or extend OpenFactory for customers. Tiers are based on certified staff, referenceable deployments and upstream contribution. The same requirements apply to every company, including companies affiliated with the project's steward.

registered

Any company that registers and accepts the code of conduct. Listed by name. May use "Powered by OpenFactory".

certified

Three certified staff including one Implementer and one Operator. One referenceable deployment. A support tier from the standard.

premier

Six certified staff including a Solution Architect. Three referenceable deployments, one Enterprise. An audited support desk. An upstream contribution record.

Specialisations

Implementation (all Certified Partners). Managed Hosting, for services that hold OpenFactory Certified Hosting. Support, for an audited desk. Training, for Authorised Training Partners with a certified trainer and a courseware licence. Technology, for add-ons listed as OpenFactory Compatible.

Partner obligations

  • Follow the delivery method on every implementation described as certified.
  • Report defects upstream and propose fixes upstream before shipping them to a second customer.
  • Keep a current roster of certified staff; staff are not shared between partners.
  • Use the marks as the trademark licence specifies.

Requirements, benefits, application and renewal: docs/program/04-partner-program.md. The partner directory will be published on this page when the first partners are listed.

Conformance

Using the OpenFactory name

Apache-2.0 grants rights to the code and no rights to the marks. Builds, hosted services and add-ons qualify for the marks by passing published conformance requirements: self-tested, submitted in public, reviewed, valid per version, and refreshed annually.

01

Certified OpenFactory: builds

The public test suite passes in both orders on the build. Every shipped provider row passes conformance-adapter. The reference project passes doctor, conformance, box prove and a rehearsal. A build may add behaviour but may not remove the quality floor, the credential boundary, the pipeline's control of version control, or the human production gate.

02

OpenFactory Certified Hosting: services

A certified build operated to the hosting standard: one deployment per customer organisation, credential boundary maintained between provider and customer, declared data location, exportable backup set, panel behind identity, version currency, tested restore, and Premium support or above. Audited annually.

03

OpenFactory Compatible: add-ons

Each declared row passes conformance-adapter on the current and previous minor release, with a support commitment on the listing.

Submission: one pull request 
v0.4/<vendor>-<product>/
PRODUCT.yaml  README.md  suite-fixed.xml  suite-random.xml

Automated checks run on submission. A program reviewer checks the participation form and the reproduction instructions within five business days. Merging the pull request constitutes certification. Listing is free for open-source distributions and non-profits. Anyone may state that a product runs on OpenFactory; the name may not be used as part of a product, service or company name.

Funding

How the program is funded

Free

  • The platform, with all features. There is no enterprise edition.
  • The documentation, including the program documents.
  • The conformance suite and the right to run it.
  • Descriptive use of the name, and conformance listing for open-source distributions and non-profits.

Paid

Certification exams, partner tiers, commercial conformance listings, courseware licences, and membership once a foundation exists. The project does not sell implementation, hosting or support, and takes no share of partner revenue.

The marks and the program transfer to a neutral holder when a second partner of comparable scale exists, and to a member foundation when three independent companies commit to membership. Until then the steward administers the program under the published documents, with conflict-of-interest rules in effect from the start.

Revenue lines, proposed fees, governance and the foundation path: docs/program/07-sustainability.md.
